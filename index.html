Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about how Exonic Technologies executes custom software development, digital marketing, and enterprise SMS campaigns.

Does Exonic provide both Software Development and Digital Marketing?
Yes! Exonic Technologies is uniquely structured as a full-service technology agency. We provide end-to-end custom software engineering, mobile apps, and cloud architectures, paired with a full-suite digital marketing team specializing in Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram), Google Ads, Social Media Management, Bulk SMS Marketing, Graphic Design, and SEO.
How do your Meta and Google Ads campaigns achieve high ROAS?
We don't rely on basic boosting. We conduct extensive audience research, build high-converting landing pages, produce high-fidelity video and graphic ad creatives, test multiple hooks dynamically, and optimize conversion tracking with Meta Conversions API and Google Enhanced Conversions to scale revenue profitably.
Who owns the source code, ad accounts, and creative assets?
You own 100% of everything. All source code, Git repositories, Figma files, branding assets, and advertising accounts remain strictly in your name. We operate transparently under strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).
How fast can our Bulk SMS and OTP gateway be deployed?
You can start sending within 24 to 48 hours. Our RESTful APIs and SMPP credentials can be integrated into your website, CRM, or mobile app in less than a day, complete with sender ID registration and delivery webhooks.
Can Exonic handle our Social Media Management and Graphic Design?
Yes. Our creative team handles your entire social media presence: creating content calendars, designing high-converting graphics and reels, scheduling daily posts on Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and Facebook, and managing community engagement.
What is your typical software development and marketing project timeline?
Marketing campaigns and landing pages can be launched in 5 to 10 days. MVP mobile and web applications typically take 4 to 8 weeks, while complex enterprise SaaS or ERP software spans 3 to 6 months in transparent 2-week agile sprints.
